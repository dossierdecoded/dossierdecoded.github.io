Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy covers the Dossier Decoded website and the Dossier Decoded publishing tool, an internal application used only by the operator of the Dossier Decoded channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. The tool is not offered to the public, and no outside user can sign in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • Our own YouTube channel, through the YouTube API Services: our uploaded videos and their metadata, our channel's analytics, and comments posted on our videos (including the commenter's public display name and comment text).
  • Our own Instagram account, through the Instagram API: our posts and their performance metrics.

The tool does not access any other person's account, private messages, contacts or personal data.

How it is used

This data is used only to publish our own videos and to understand which videos and topics our audience finds most interesting, so we can plan future episodes. Public comments may be read to find topics viewers have requested.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through these APIs with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is stored in a private repository accessible only to the operator.

Retention and deletion

Analytics and comment data are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. To have a comment you wrote removed from our records, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our YouTube content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access granted to this tool can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's own privacy statement.
